Welcome to Norwich wildFinder!

Discover the Biodiversity of Norwich

A growing community of citizen scientists are using iNaturalist ,one of the worlds most popular nature apps, to log a remarkable diversity of wildlife across Norwich. You can use the Norwich wildFinder to easily browse their observations on a map. To get started, use the menu on the right to select a wildlife group that interests you — choose from birds, insects, plants, fungi, mammals and more and then click the Show Map button to see these observations plotted on the map. At the initial zoom level the map displays a dark blue grid showing where sightings for your chosen wildife group are concentrated across the area. As you zoom in close individual sighting markers will appear; click any marker to see details of the species recorded at that location. Click the scientific name to open a species search in wikipedia to find out more, and click the iNaturalist link to see more details of that particular observation and the person that made it. You can open and close the side panel using the menu button if you want a fuller view of the map.

Become a Citizen Scientist

Why not become a citizen scientist! nature needs you — and you don't have to be an expert to make an important difference! By downloading the free iNaturalist app on your smartphone, you can record the plants and animals you encounter on your walks through Norwich. Every photo you snap and share helps us understand and protect our local environment. Any observations you record on iNaturalist will automatically appear on this Norwich wildFinder map.
